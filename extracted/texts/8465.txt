Just a few more points before I let you get on with the conquering, sire. Nine other kingdoms occupy this map, each of them shown in the faction key on the upper left. You can expand the faction key to see each kingdom&apos;s current income and victory point total. Victory points (or VPs) are used to determine the ultimate victor, so conquering the entire map is not necessary. You gain one point for each region you control and two points for each rival kingdom you eliminate. Once you reach the target number of victory points, you must hold them for five turns before final victory is declared.
